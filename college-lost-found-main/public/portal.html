How College Lost & Found Works

Our system connects people who have lost items with people who have found them.

1

Lost Something?

Go to the Report Lost section and enter the details of your missing item, such as its name, description, location and date.

2

Automatic Matching

Your report is stored in the database. When someone reports a found item, the system checks the submitted details for a possible match.

3

A Match Is Found

If the system detects a strong match, the relevant report will provide the contact information needed to connect the two people.

4

Found Something?

Go to the Report Found section and submit the item's details along with its photo. The system will check it against existing lost-item reports.
